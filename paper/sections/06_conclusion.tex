\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A validity rate for prompt-enforced structured output hides most of what
a practitioner needs. Across \pn{reported.models} models, models with
similar rates fail in different ways; a model's dominant category moves
when the constraints that bind it change; and the most common failure,
markdown wrapping, conceals a length or pattern violation often enough
that reading primary labels alone misreads a prompt ablation. Whether a
relaxation removes a failure depends on the sub-cause beneath the label,
not on the label. We release the classifier, raw completions and
annotation record so the corpus can be relabelled under other
definitions; the distribution over categories will move as models
change, and the vocabulary is meant to outlast it.
