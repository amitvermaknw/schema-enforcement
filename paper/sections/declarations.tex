\section*{Statements and Declarations}
\label{sec:declarations}

\bmhead{Funding} No funding was received for this work. API usage was
paid for by the author.

\bmhead{Competing interests} The author declares no competing interests.
The author has no financial ties to OpenAI or Anthropic, whose models are
evaluated in this study. The author works as an on-site contractor at
American Express through Data Systems Integration Group; this work was
carried out independently and is unrelated to that role.

\bmhead{Ethics approval} Not applicable. The study involves no human
participants; the datasets used are public.

\bmhead{Consent to participate and consent for publication} Not applicable.

\bmhead{Data availability} The results database, raw completions, the
development and held-out annotations, and the question manifest are
available at \url{https://github.com/amitvermaknw/schema-enforcement} and
archived at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23586834}.
FinanceBench text is redistributed under CC~BY-NC~4.0 and HotpotQA
questions under CC~BY-SA~4.0 (see \code{DATA\_LICENSES.md}).

\bmhead{Code availability} The harness, classifier and analysis scripts
are available in the same repository.

\bmhead{Use of AI tools}
The author used large language model assistants (Claude) for writing,
editing and coding assistance, and, during classifier development, as an
advisor when interpreting model outputs for the development labels;
every label was assigned by the author. The held-out labels were assigned
by the author without AI assistance on any item; an assistant was
consulted only on the wording of the category definitions. All
experiments were designed, run and verified by the author; every number
reported is generated by scripts from the released results database.

\bmhead{Author contributions} Sole author: conceptualisation,
methodology, software, analysis and writing.
